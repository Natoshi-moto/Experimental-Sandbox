# Experimental Sandbox charter

## Mission

Maximise legitimate public experimentation while making accidental contamination of Nexus Lab mechanically difficult.

## Active operator holds

Before acting, read [`EMERGENCY_CURRENT_STATUS.md`](EMERGENCY_CURRENT_STATUS.md).
A dated written operator hold may temporarily narrow official Sandbox actions.
It outranks loose activation language, old branches, open pull requests, and
passing checks within its stated scope. It does not create scientific, legal,
canonical, or Nexus Lab authority.

The current hold is [`SBX-SOH-001`](operations/operator-holds/SBX-SOH-001/ORDER.md).
Unrelated experiments remain governed by the normal zones below.

## Zones

| Zone | Meaning | Normal behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `main` | Append-only public index, templates and accepted experiment records | PRs and checks |
| `sandbox/thought/*` | Raw ideas and articles | Fast, disposable |
| `sandbox/experiment/*` | Named experiments | Evidence encouraged |
| `sandbox/fork/*` | Research forks | Provenance and rights declaration required |
| `sandbox/reproduction/*` | Independent or differential reruns | Preserve disagreement |
| `sandbox/break/*` | Adversarial attacks | Attack claims and mechanisms, not people |

Branches may fail or be abandoned. Published evidence must not be silently rewritten to manufacture a better result.

## Intellectual posture

- Ambition is not a defect.
- Profanity is not evidence, but it is not disqualifying.
- Discomfort, controversy and criticism are not safety failures by themselves.
- A hedge must name the uncertainty, consequence, evidence needed and safest ambitious path.
- Several AIs agreeing is not independent corroboration.
- Failed experiments are course material.

## Hard boundaries

- No secrets.
- No doxxing, threats or targeted harassment.
- No unsupported impersonation or affiliation claims.
- No experiment workflow receives credentials capable of writing Nexus Lab.
- No submodule or subtree path into Nexus Lab.
- No automatic canonical promotion.
- No bypass of an active written operator hold.
- No affected branch or pull request merges without the hold classification it requires.
