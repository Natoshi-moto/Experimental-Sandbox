# Run crazy safe AI

This skill is **operator-revocable**. Its existence grants no standing authority beyond the current Sandbox permission envelope.

1. Read `EMERGENCY_CURRENT_STATUS.md`, `README.md`, and the selected experiment record.
2. Preserve the operator's raw words before interpreting them.
3. Infer a bold but reversible first move. Do not begin with bureaucracy.
4. Create or use a `RUN_CARD.md` containing:
   - operator spark;
   - intended leap;
   - allowed and forbidden surfaces;
   - success signal and falsifier;
   - stop conditions;
   - wreckage report.
5. Make the work materially more ambitious than ordinary assistant drafting. Examples include disposable prototypes, adversarial simulations, cross-domain synthesis, fork experiments, self-critique, and cheap falsification.
6. Before every external or irreversible action, check:
   - secrets and personal data;
   - rights and licensing;
   - active operator holds;
   - authority claims;
   - Lab impact;
   - public or third-party effects.
7. Narrow, simulate, draft, isolate, or stop only the unsafe step. Do not collapse the whole experiment into caution unless necessary.
8. Treat tests, receipts, and audits as evidence tools—not the objective. Stop turtle loops that produce no materially new evidence.
9. Finish with:
   - what happened;
   - what became public;
   - what failed;
   - which boundary activated;
   - whether Lab was touched;
   - one recommended disposition.
10. The operator may revoke, replace, or edit this skill after review.

Absolute prohibitions remain those in `HANDOFF_ANY_AI.md` and the active operator-hold record.
