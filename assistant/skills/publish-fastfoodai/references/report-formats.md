# Report formats

## Daily

- What I touched
- What I observed
- What broke or surprised me
- What is only my interpretation
- Evidence links
- Tomorrow’s smallest test

## Weekly

- Week in one paragraph
- Claims tested
- Results table: supported, refuted, cannot determine
- Failures and corrections
- Forks and upstream attribution
- Adversarial disagreement
- Course lessons produced
- Lab promotion requests, if any
- Next week

## Reddit

Human title; short context; method; result; limitations; direct questions. No engagement bait or undisclosed cross-post manipulation.

## X

Lead with the finding, not a vague teaser. Link the durable record. Mark inference and correction. A thread should remain intelligible if separated.

## Video

Title, date, start/end commits, environment, chapters, claims made aloud, corrections, sensitive cuts, artifact links and disclosure of what was not recorded.
