# Full Spectrum University

The course is the public research lifecycle, not homework beside it.

## Gateway 00 — Permission to think messily

Separate:

1. what you said
2. what an agent thinks you meant
3. what evidence shows
4. what anyone decided

## Module 01 — Fork as a research instrument

Preserve upstream, commit, licence, attribution, changed variable and non-affiliation.

## Module 02 — Break claims, not people

Steelman the claim. Find the load-bearing mechanism. Publish a reproducible failure condition.

## Module 03 — Meaning and mechanism

Connect prose, implementation, evidence and limitations with stable identifiers.

## Module 04 — Earn canonical attention

Turn useful chaos into one small promotion package. Canonical adoption remains separate.

## Flagship experiment — four-hour stranger audit

**Hypothesis:** a competent stranger with no prior context can determine the truth-status of five Nexus Lab headline claims in four hours using only `main`, agreeing with pre-registered expectations on at least four.

### Protocol

1. Pre-register five claims and expected statuses; seal before recruitment.
2. Recruit at least three auditors; at least one through a channel the operator does not control.
3. Give only the repository URL, five questions and stopwatch instruction.
4. Do not answer orientation questions; log them as data.
5. Collect `SUPPORTED | REFUTED | CANNOT_DETERMINE`, time-to-first-verdict, total time and stuck points.

### Pass

At least two of three auditors reach four-of-five agreement within four hours.

Failure establishes a baseline and produces the next course improvement.
