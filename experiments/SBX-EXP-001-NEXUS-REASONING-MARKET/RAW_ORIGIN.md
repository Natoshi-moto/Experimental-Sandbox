## Interpretation notes

- “one big” is ambiguous in the source sentence. This experiment does not
  silently repair it into a stronger claim.
- “anonymous” is treated as a desired property to test, not a property already
  available. V0 may expose pseudonymous aliases but must not claim
  unlinkability.
- “token” is constrained to `SIM_CREDIT` until a separately authorized
  real-value programme clears specialist review and a clean genesis.
- “one clean transaction” is interpreted as one logical Job Capsule and one
  atomic terminal settlement, not an hours-long database transaction.
- “verified on GitHub” means GitHub records a mechanically replayable witness
  capsule. GitHub does not become the ledger or prove semantic correctness.
