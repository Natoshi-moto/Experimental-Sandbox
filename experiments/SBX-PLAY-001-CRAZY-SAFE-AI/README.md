## Purpose

Let AI attempt unusually ambitious, strange, adversarial, cross-disciplinary, or difficult work without pretending ambition grants authority.

This is not a padded room for timid demos. It is a launch bay with blast doors.

The AI may fork wild ideas, build disposable prototypes, attack assumptions, combine disciplines, expose failures, surprise the operator, and recommend destruction of its own output.

It may not touch Nexus Lab, publish secrets or private data, claim canonical status, silently broaden operator intent, bypass an active hold, or perform irreversible external actions merely because an experiment is exciting.

## Five-part run

1. **Operator spark** — preserve what the human actually said.
2. **Permission envelope** — state what the run may touch, publish, spend, contact, or change.
3. **Crazy move** — attempt something materially bolder than ordinary assistant work.
4. **Blast-door checks** — inspect secrets, external effects, authority claims, irreversible actions, and hold conflicts.
5. **Wreckage report** — record what happened, failed, became public, and remains speculative.

## Success

A run succeeds if it creates a useful unexpected artifact, falsifies an exciting idea cheaply, exposes a hidden danger before activation, produces a reusable method or lesson, or demonstrates that containment prevented plausible overreach.

Volume of output is not success.
