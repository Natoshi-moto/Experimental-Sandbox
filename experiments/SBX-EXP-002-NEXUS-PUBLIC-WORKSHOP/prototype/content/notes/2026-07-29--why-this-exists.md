# Why this workshop exists

NEXUS Public Workshop is where the writing, software, evidence, and unanswered questions live together.

The point is not to manufacture the appearance of a finished institution. It
is to let people see the position, inspect the artefacts, try what genuinely
works, and tell the difference between a result and an ambition.

Everything important should be traceable from the claim back to the work that
produced it.

## The publishing rule

Claims get labels before they get attention. A passing test proves only what it
tested. A prototype is not a deployment. A proposal is not policy.

Known limitations belong beside the evidence, not buried beneath it.
