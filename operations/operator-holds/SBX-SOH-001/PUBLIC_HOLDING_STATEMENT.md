# Public holding statement — SBX-SOH-001

I have placed a temporary operator hold on the Sandbox internal-credit and
agent-economy direction.

I am considering a durable design in which users may eventually hold and
transfer internal credits and use them only to obtain AI intelligence or AI
work inside the system. I have **not** authorised that design, a live credit,
participant transfers, recruitment, or a launch.

I will not pretend that a transferable credit which buys actual AI work is
valueless. It would have functional utility, and people may try to trade that
utility privately. I also will not allow that fact, by itself, to force this
project into becoming a financial product or to dictate every useful design
choice.

What the official project can control is its own conduct: what it issues, what
its infrastructure supports, what it markets, what it promises, what it
accepts into official collaboration, and how it responds to abuse within
project-controlled surfaces. It cannot truthfully claim control over all
private conduct, independent forks, or off-platform agreements.

The hold keeps research from outrunning responsibility while I reconstruct:

- the purpose I am committing to;
- the boundaries that are permanent;
- the mechanisms that remain replaceable;
- the powers I personally retain;
- the conduct the official project will refuse to facilitate;
- the evidence and appeal standard for enforcement; and
- the facts that would require pause, redesign, or abandonment.

Research may continue as documentation, adversarial analysis, comparison, and
falsification of the frozen simulator. Implementation, activation, live
transfer, participant recruitment, and claims of final approval may not.

This is a line in the sand about process and responsibility. It is not the
final verdict on the design.
