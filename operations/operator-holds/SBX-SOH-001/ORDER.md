# SBX-SOH-001 — temporary operator hold on the internal-credit and agent-economy direction

**State:** `ACTIVE`
**Effective date:** `2026-07-27`
**Activated by:** `HUMAN_OPERATOR_RING_0`
**Operator process authority:** `SANDBOX_OPERATOR_HOLD`
**Scientific/canonical status authority:** `NONE`
**Nexus Lab impact:** `NONE`

## 1. Purpose

This order prevents research concerning internal credits, agent economies,
community allocation, and AI-work purchasing from being mistaken for:

- an accepted economic design;
- an authorised implementation;
- a token or credit launch;
- an invitation to participate;
- a promise of value, conversion, compensation, or future entitlement;
- a settled operator moral or political position; or
- a proposal accepted by Nexus Lab.

The operator is considering a durable project direction and a personal public
position. Those choices are not yet reconstructed precisely enough to govern a
live system.

This hold prevents premature commitment. It does not suppress scrutiny.

## 2. Scope

This order governs official Experimental Sandbox activity involving:

- participant-facing internal credits;
- the ability to hold or transfer such credits;
- credits that could purchase actual AI intelligence or AI work;
- community funding or allocation through those credits;
- governance, reputation, access, priority, or bargaining power attached to
  them;
- claims about external value, redemption, exchange, or financial status;
- enforcement against misuse of official project surfaces; and
- any request to promote the held direction toward Nexus Lab.

It applies to new work and to existing branches, pull requests, documents,
interfaces, agents, and experiments when they touch that scope.

It does not apply to unrelated Sandbox research.

## 3. Immediate prohibitions

Until this order is explicitly lifted, narrowed, or superseded, no official
Sandbox action may:

1. launch, issue, distribute, mint, allocate, redeem, or activate a
   participant-facing credit;
2. enable live participant-to-participant credit transfers;
3. connect a credit to actual model execution or AI-work purchasing for real
   participants;
4. recruit users, contributors, testers, funders, purchasers, or communities
   into a live credit economy;
5. represent any proposed credit mechanism as accepted project architecture or
   final operator policy;
6. add fiat or crypto conversion, external-goods purchasing, price discovery,
   liquidity, yield, interest, staking return, collateralisation, derivatives,
   debt, or external settlement;
7. market a credit as an investment, savings product, income, ownership,
   equity, appreciation opportunity, or promise of future conversion;
8. claim that a future credit capable of purchasing actual AI work has no
   functional utility;
9. claim that official rules can completely prevent private barter, sale,
   lending, collateralisation, or other off-platform arrangements;
10. merge mechanism expansion or activation while describing it as merely
    research maintenance;
11. promote the held economic direction to Nexus Lab; or
12. treat voice discussion, model agreement, an old branch, an open pull
    request, a green check, popularity, or silence as operator authorisation.

## 4. Work that may continue

The following work may continue when clearly labelled as research and when it
creates no participant-facing economy:

- preservation, indexing, and provenance work;
- threat modelling and abuse analysis;
- adversarial review and falsification;
- social, economic, governance, concentration, coercion, privacy, and informal
  market analysis;
- comparison of transferable, non-transferable, expiring, account-bound,
  project-budget, receipt-only, and no-token alternatives;
- analysis of whether an internal credit is necessary at all;
- reconstruction of the operator decision and responsibility boundary;
- drafting of the press pack, public explanation, FAQ, enforcement standard,
  powers disclosure, and appeal route;
- documentation of disagreements and unresolved questions; and
- execution of the already-frozen `SBX-EXP-001` simulator solely to verify,
  reproduce, or attack its existing claims.

Permitted research does not authorize feature expansion, real model purchase,
real participants, live transfer, production adapters, or economic launch.

## 5. The current simulator and the contemplated design are different

The committed `SIM_CREDIT` in `SBX-EXP-001` is closed simulation data. It does
not buy actual AI work and has no bridge, redemption, withdrawal, exchange, or
external settlement adapter.

The operator is separately considering—but has not authorised—a future design
in which an internal credit might be held, transferred, and spent only on AI
intelligence or AI work inside the system.

These must not be conflated.

A future service credit would have functional utility because it could obtain
AI work. The official project may reject external redemption, financial
returns, external goods, exchange infrastructure, and speculative marketing.
Those limits do not make functional utility disappear.

## 6. Private exchange cannot be eliminated by declaration

If a system permits user-to-user transfer, a private sale can occur without an
official exchange: consideration may move elsewhere and the credit may move
inside the project.

The project can refuse to facilitate that conduct, avoid payment integrations,
avoid official exchange rates, restrict project-controlled accounts or
services under a published rule, and reject speculative promotion. It cannot
truthfully promise complete control over private agreements, independent
forks, or conduct outside project-controlled infrastructure.

The final design must confront rather than hide the trade-offs among:

- transferability;
- user autonomy;
- privacy;
- resistance to informal markets;
- concentration controls;
- monitoring and enforcement; and
- exclusion power.

## 7. Repository history is evidence, not adequate current disclosure

The public Git history may evidence the development of the operator's intent.
It is not sufficient to require an ordinary participant to reconstruct every
material boundary from Genesis onward.

Material risks, powers, limits, and non-guarantees must also appear in a clear,
current, plain-language statement at the project's entry points.

## 8. Operator responsibility under deliberation

The final position must state whether and to what extent the operator accepts
responsibility for:

- accurate framing;
- foreseeable public interpretation;
- refusing investment and redemption promises;
- distinguishing research, simulation, proposal, accepted state, and live
  operation;
- designing against obvious abuse and exploitation;
- disclosing exceptional powers and conflicts of interest;
- enforcing rules within official project surfaces;
- documenting material failures;
- pausing or changing mechanisms that contradict the stated purpose; and
- making material boundaries accessible without repository archaeology.

This order makes no claim that intent alone discharges those responsibilities.

## 9. Enforcement boundary

The official project may control:

- project-operated accounts and services;
- acceptance, review, and merge of official contributions;
- official repositories, branches, releases, documentation, and names;
- official transfer or allocation functions; and
- participation in official governance processes.

It cannot control all forks, private agreements, external speech, unofficial
markets, or downstream uses of public outputs.

Any future restriction or exclusion policy must be published, evidence-based,
proportionate, documented, consistently applied, limited to surfaces the
project controls, and paired with a review or appeal route. Suspicion alone is
not a sufficient standard.

## 10. Existing branches and pull requests

The one change that installs this order is classified
`OPERATOR_AUTHORIZED_HOLD_ACTIVATION`. Its authority is the written record in
[`RAW_OPERATOR_AUTHORIZATION.md`](RAW_OPERATOR_AUTHORIZATION.md). That exception
authorizes only the hold and its enforcement surfaces; it authorizes no held
economic mechanism.

Every other existing branch or pull request touching the held scope is
`NO_MERGE` until it records one of these classifications:

- `OUT_OF_SCOPE` — no held mechanism or public-position effect;
- `ALLOWED_RESEARCH_ONLY` — preservation, verification, falsification,
  documentary, or adversarial work only; or
- `BLOCKED_BY_SBX-SOH-001` — implementation, expansion, activation, live
  transfer, participant recruitment, financialisation, or premature public
  commitment.

Classification does not itself lift the hold. A blocked change requires a
later dated operator decision.

## 11. Required release package

The hold may be lifted only after reviewable versions exist of:

1. an operator decision record stating what is being chosen and why;
2. a permanent-boundaries statement;
3. a revisable-design statement;
4. a plain-language public explanation;
5. a risk and non-guarantee statement;
6. a technical boundary specification;
7. an enforcement and appeal policy;
8. an operator-powers and conflicts disclosure;
9. an adversarial review instructed to attack the mechanism and wording; and
10. an explicit dated written operator instruction identifying what is
    authorised.

Completion of a document, branch, test suite, or review is evidence for the
operator. It is not self-executing approval.

## 12. Lift, narrowing, or supersession

Only the Human Operator may lift, narrow, or supersede this order, and only in
dated writing.

No model, contributor, maintainer, vote, implied consensus, voice transcript,
open PR, passing check, or repository convention may do so on the operator's
behalf.

## 13. Non-effects

This order:

- does not delete or rewrite prior research;
- does not declare the proposed system lawful or unlawful;
- does not accuse any participant of misconduct;
- does not reject the underlying mission;
- does not endorse transferability or any specific credit mechanism;
- does not create a credit, token, financial instrument, participant right, or
  future entitlement;
- does not establish Nexus Lab accepted state; and
- does not convert the operator's continuing deliberation into a final verdict.
