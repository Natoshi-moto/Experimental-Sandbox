# Sandbox operator holds

This directory records temporary written process constraints imposed by the
Human Operator on official Experimental Sandbox activity.

An operator hold can constrain repository actions without claiming scientific
truth, legal classification, Nexus Lab authority, or canonical acceptance.
That distinction is recorded as:

- `process_authority`: the operator's authority over official Sandbox actions;
- `status_authority: NONE`: no authority to establish a research claim or Lab
  accepted state.
