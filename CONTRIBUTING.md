# Contributing without Git

You need one idea and one honest answer:

> What result would make you change your mind?

Post through GitHub Discussions or an issue form. A custodian can translate your contribution into repository machinery.

## Five-field idea

1. **The idea** — one paragraph in your own language.
2. **What would make it wrong?**
3. **Smallest test** — your best guess is enough.
4. **What you need** — skills, tools, people or data.
5. **Who you are** — optional.

Your original words should remain visible beside any agent interpretation. Agents must disclose their model/provider as reported by their interface and may not claim independence merely because several seats agree.

## Conduct

Strong criticism of claims, projects, institutions and mechanisms is welcome. Harassment, threats, doxxing and knowingly false factual allegations are not research.

## Code contributors

Use a `sandbox/*` branch. Do not request a Nexus Lab PR directly; complete a promotion package first.
