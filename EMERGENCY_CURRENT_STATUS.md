## THE LINE IS HELD

The proposed internal-credit, agent-economy, community-allocation, and
AI-work direction is under a temporary operator hold.

This is **not** a blanket shutdown of Experimental Sandbox and it is **not** a
rejection of the research. It prevents exploratory material from becoming an
official economic commitment, participant-facing system, public launch, or
Lab proposal before the operator has published the governing position,
boundaries, powers, enforcement standard, and failure conditions.

The full order is [`operations/operator-holds/SBX-SOH-001/ORDER.md`](operations/operator-holds/SBX-SOH-001/ORDER.md).
The machine-readable state is [`STATUS.json`](operations/operator-holds/SBX-SOH-001/STATUS.json).

## Effective immediately

No official Sandbox action may, within the held scope:

- launch, issue, allocate, redeem, or activate participant-facing credits;
- enable live participant-to-participant credit transfers;
- recruit people into a live credit economy;
- merge mechanism expansion or activation as approved project direction;
- add fiat, crypto, external-goods, exchange, liquidity, yield, staking,
  collateral, price-discovery, or financial-return machinery;
- describe a future credit that purchases actual AI work as having “no value”;
- claim private off-platform exchange can be completely prevented; or
- present the operator's deliberation as a final design verdict.

Any existing branch or pull request touching the held scope is **no-merge**
until it is explicitly classified against `SBX-SOH-001`. Unrelated Sandbox
work may continue normally.

## Research that may continue

Preservation, indexing, threat modelling, adversarial review, social and
economic failure analysis, comparative mechanism research, documentation,
press-pack preparation, and verification of the already-frozen simulator may
continue.

That permission does not authorize feature expansion, live activation, real
participants, real model purchasing, or transferable service credits.

## Plain truth that must survive publication

The current `SIM_CREDIT` prototype is frozen simulation data and buys no actual
service. A future credit capable of buying real AI intelligence or AI work
would have functional utility. Official non-redemption and anti-market design
can constrain project-controlled behavior; they cannot make that utility
disappear or guarantee that no private exchange will occur.

## Lift condition

Only a dated written instruction from the Human Operator may lift, narrow, or
supersede this hold. Voice discussion, model output, silence, continued
research, an open branch, a pull request, a passing test, or repository
popularity cannot do so.
